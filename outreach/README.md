# Outreach Materials for Data Days

This directory contains templates and materials for promoting the Data Days initiative at the MIT Koch Institute. These materials are designed to help you effectively communicate the value of Data Days and the Science Data Kit (SDK) to potential participants.

## Available Materials

### Email Templates
- [Announcement Email](email_announcement.md) - Template for initial announcement of upcoming Data Days events
- [Reminder Email](email_announcement.md#follow-up-email-1-week-before-event) - Template for reminder emails to send closer to the event

### Promotional Materials
- [Flyer Template](flyer_template.md) - Design and content guidelines for creating printable flyers and digital posters

### Event Planning
- [Calendar Invite Template](calendar_invite.md) - Template for creating calendar invitations with all necessary event details
- [Slack Announcement Templates](slack_announcement.md) - Templates for announcing events on Slack channels

## How to Use These Materials

1. **Choose the appropriate template** for your communication channel and audience
2. **Customize the content** with specific event details, dates, times, and locations
3. **Adjust the technical level** based on your target audience
4. **Add relevant examples** specific to the recipient's research area
5. **Include clear calls to action** with registration links and contact information

## Customization Tips

### For Different Audiences

- **Trainees**: Emphasize workshopping datasets to develop protocols for handling different types of data and curating datasets for specific uses
- **PIs and Lab Administrators**: Focus on improved data organization, collaboration, and research efficiency
- **Technical Audiences**: Highlight the Neo4j backend, knowledge graph capabilities, and technical architecture
- **Non-Technical Audiences**: Emphasize the intuitive interface and practical benefits

### For Different Research Areas

- **Imaging Labs**: Highlight metadata extraction from microscopy files and image organization features
- **Genomics Research**: Focus on organizing sequencing data and related metadata
- **Clinical Research**: Emphasize data privacy, compliance features, and patient data organization

## Distribution Strategy

For maximum reach, consider using multiple channels:

1. **Email**: Send personalized emails to department mailing lists and individual researchers
2. **Physical Flyers**: Post in department common areas, near lab spaces, and on bulletin boards
3. **Digital Promotion**: Share on department websites, digital signage, and social media
4. **Calendar Invites**: Send to confirmed participants and interested parties
5. **Slack Announcements**: Post in relevant Slack channels and communities

## Timeline Recommendations

For optimal promotion, follow this timeline:

- **3-4 weeks before**: Send initial announcement emails and post flyers
- **2 weeks before**: Post Slack announcements and send calendar invites
- **1 week before**: Send reminder emails and post reminder Slack messages
- **1-2 days before**: Send final reminder emails and Slack messages
- **After the event**: Send follow-up emails with resources and information about future events

## Measuring Effectiveness

To improve your outreach efforts, consider tracking:

- Email open and click-through rates
- Registration numbers from different channels
- Attendance rates
- Post-event survey responses about how participants heard about the event

## Customizing Materials

All templates are provided in Markdown format for easy editing. To customize:

1. Copy the template file
2. Edit with any text editor or Markdown editor
3. Replace placeholder text with your specific event details
4. Add or remove sections as needed for your specific context
