## Main Content

### What is Data Days?

An open house initiative at the MIT Koch Institute where researchers can bring their experimental datasets and learn to organize and structure them using our Science Data Kit (SDK).

### Why Attend?

- **Organize** your messy research files
- **Extract** valuable metadata automatically
- **Visualize** your data in interactive dashboards
- **Discover** connections between datasets
- **Query** your data using natural language

### Who Should Attend?

- **Trainees** working in the thick of data who want to simplify aggregating information and recalculating analyses
- **PIs & Lab Managers** seeking better data organization
- **Anyone** interested in improving research data management

### What to Bring

- Your laptop
- Research data files you'd like to organize
- Questions about data management

## Featured Technology

### Science Data Kit (SDK)

Our toolkit uses a knowledge graph model to help you:

- Scan and organize research files
- Extract metadata from various file formats
- Map entities and relationships
- Create interactive visualizations
- Interact with your data using natural language

- Secondary: Dark Gray (#333333)

- Background: Light Gray (#F2F2F2)

### Typography
- Headings: Arial Bold or Helvetica Neue Bold

- Include the MIT Koch Institute logo

### Layout Suggestions

## Customization Tips

1. **Tailor to specific departments** by including examples relevant to their research
2. **Highlight specific features** based on the target audience:

4. **Include specific success stories** from previous Data Days events
