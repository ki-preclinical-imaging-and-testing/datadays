### Who Should Attend?

Data Days is designed for:

- **Principal Investigators and Lab Administrators** interested in better data organization
- **Anyone** looking to improve their research data management skills

No prior experience with data management tools is required. Just bring your laptop and some research data you'd like to organize!

### What is the Science Data Kit?

The Science Data Kit (SDK) is a toolkit we've developed that uses a knowledge graph model backed by Neo4j to provide:

- Automated scanning and organization of research files
- Metadata extraction from various file formats
- Entity mapping and relationship modeling
- Interactive visualization dashboards
- Natural language interaction with your data

The SDK is built in Python using Streamlit and integrates with Docker-based Neo4j, Jupyter Lab, and NeoDash for visualization.
